Hot chocolate that heats itself, right in the can

Velvet Ember is a self-heating hot chocolate can in development. Activate the base, wait for the sealed heating chamber to warm the drink, then pull the tab. No kettle, microwave or café needed.

Discover Velvet Ember How does self-heating work?

A warm drink, without a kettle in sight

Hostels with no hot water. Late library sessions. A long train ride. A cold evening outdoors. These are the moments when a hot chocolate sounds perfect and making one is impractical.

Velvet Ember puts the drink and the heat source in the same can. The heat comes from a well-understood reaction: when water meets calcium oxide (quicklime), it releases heat. That reaction happens in a separately sealed chamber beneath the drink, and the warmth travels up through an aluminium wall into your hot chocolate.

Quick answers

What is Velvet Ember?

Velvet Ember is a self-heating hot chocolate can being developed in India. It pairs a 230 ml hot chocolate section with a separately sealed heating chamber, so you can warm the drink without a kettle, microwave or café.

Is Velvet Ember available to buy?

Not yet. Velvet Ember is a pre-launch product. Price, launch cities and sales channels are still being confirmed, and nothing is sold through this website today.

Does the heating material touch the drink?

It’s designed not to. The calcium oxide and water sit inside a sealed aluminium cell in the lower chamber, and heat passes through the cell wall into the hot chocolate. Independent test evidence for the final can will be published once available.
