Velvet Ember Self-Heating Hot Chocolate Can

A portable can with a hot chocolate section on top and a separately sealed heating chamber underneath. Designed to give you a warm chocolate drink anywhere, without a separate appliance.

Get launch updatesHow does self-heating work?

In short

Velvet Ember is a 420 ml self-heating can in development. Its upper 230 ml section holds hot chocolate. Its lower 190 ml chamber holds a sealed aluminium cell with 69 g of food-grade calcium oxide and a 60 ml pouch of distilled water. Activating the base lets the water reach the calcium oxide, which releases heat that warms the drink through the cell wall.

The drink and the heating materials are designed to stay in separately sealed sections. You drink from a standard pull-tab lid on top.

What it’s designed to do for you

No appliance needed
The heat source is built into the can, so you don’t need hot water, a kettle or a microwave.
One thing to carry
Drink, heater and lid in a single can that fits a bag.
Separate by design
The heating materials sit in their own sealed chamber, away from the drink.
Familiar finish
You open it and drink from it like any pull-tab can.

Questions about the can

How much hot chocolate is in one can?

The upper section is designed as a 230 ml payload area. The exact net drink volume will be confirmed on the final label.

How long does it take to heat?

We haven’t published a heat-up time yet. Earlier planning figures differ, so we’re validating one through testing and will print it on the can and publish it here.

Can I reuse the can?

No. It’s designed as a single-use can. Disposal and recycling guidance will be published before launch.
