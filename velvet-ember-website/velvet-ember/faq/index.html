Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about Velvet Ember, including the things we haven’t confirmed yet.

About the product

What is Velvet Ember?

Velvet Ember is a self-heating hot chocolate can being developed in India. It pairs a 230 ml hot chocolate section with a separately sealed heating chamber, so you can warm the drink without a kettle, microwave or café.

Who is it for?

We’re designing it first for students and young professionals in India who want a warm drink away from home: in hostels, on campus, while travelling or outdoors.

What flavours are there?

One hot chocolate variant is planned. Any other flavours or pack sizes haven’t been confirmed.

Heating and use

How does the can heat itself?

A sealed lower chamber holds calcium oxide and a pouch of water. Activating the base pierces the pouch; the water reacts with the calcium oxide and releases heat, which passes through an aluminium wall into the drink. See how it works.

How do I activate it?

The current design activates from the base, which pierces the internal water pouch. The exact consumer steps are being finalised with safety testing and will be printed on every can. Always follow the printed instructions.

How long does heating take?

We haven’t published a heat-up time. Earlier planning documents give different figures, so we’re confirming one through testing before we state it.

How long does it stay warm?

This is being tested. We’ll publish a validated figure before launch.

How hot does the drink get?

We don’t yet have validated temperature results for the final can, so we’re not making a temperature claim.

Safety and ingredients

Is the heating material kept separate from the drink?

Yes, by design. The calcium oxide and water are sealed inside an aluminium cell in the lower chamber, apart from the hot chocolate. Test evidence for the final can will be shared once available.

Is the can safe to handle?

Safety testing of the final design is in progress, and we won’t claim the can is safe to handle until test results support it. Handling guidance will be printed on the can.

What ingredients and allergens does it contain?

The draft recipe specifies Callebaut or Cacao Barry cocoa. The final ingredient list, allergen information and nutrition panel are not ready yet and will be published before launch.

Is it approved by FSSAI?

Not yet claimed. The required food (FSSAI), consumer-safety (BIS) and logistics (PESO) clearances are being worked through. We’ll list each one here only once it’s granted.

What is the shelf life?

Shelf life hasn’t been confirmed. It will appear on the label once stability testing is complete.

Buying and disposal

Can I buy it now?

Not yet. Velvet Ember is a pre-launch product. Price, launch cities and sales channels are still being confirmed, and nothing is sold through this website today.

What will it cost?

The proposed price is around ₹220 per can (MRP). It may change before launch.

Where will it be available?

Quick-commerce apps, online marketplaces, our own web store and selected shops are being explored. Launch cities aren’t confirmed. Get launch updates to hear first.

How should I dispose of or recycle it?

Guidance is being prepared, because the used can contains a spent heating module as well as aluminium. We’ll publish clear instructions before launch.
