# 01 — Market, competitor and keyword research

Research date: 17 Nov 2026. Web research was used; no paid keyword tool was available, so **no search volumes or difficulty scores are given**. Priorities below are qualitative. Pull real volumes from Google Keyword Planner (India), Google Search Console (after launch) and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## 1. Category landscape (verified via public sources)
- Self-heating cans are an old, repeatedly attempted category. The idea dates to 1897 (The Conversation, 2018). Nestlé trialled a 210 ml self-heating coffee in the UK in 2001 that rose ~40 °C above ambient in about 3 minutes; Wolfgang Puck's 2005 self-heating latte used OnTech's calcium-oxide heater (Daily Coffee News, 2018; Make:, 2008).
- Common failure points cited in coverage: cost, space taken by the heater, uneven/lukewarm heating, and historic safety concerns.
- 2020s activity is led by solid-state heaters (HeatGen tech used by La Colombe and High Brew Coffee: twist base, ~2 minutes, ~130 °F reported by press).
- India: Extovate Ventures (Bengaluru) develops self-heating/cooling pouches, heating in under 10 minutes per Packaging South Asia. Hotbon was a separate Bengaluru-area self-heating container startup covered in 2015.

**Opportunity:** no clear consumer brand owns "self-heating hot chocolate" in India. Content explaining the mechanism and safety plainly fills a real gap; most coverage is Western coffee-focused.

## 3. Competitor content patterns
Competitor product pages lead with heat-up time and temperature, a 3-step activation graphic, and lifestyle use (camping, commuting). Gaps: little plain-language chemistry, thin safety/disposal information, almost no transparency about what's tested. Velvet Ember's differentiator on-site: an explicit "what we're still confirming" ledger, mechanism explainer, and sourced learn content.
