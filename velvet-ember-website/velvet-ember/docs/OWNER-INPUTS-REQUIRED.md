# Owner inputs required before publishing

Nothing below should be stated on the site until resolved. The site currently shows these as **pending**.

## Blocking for launch
1. **Heat-up time: 1 minute vs 3 minutes.** The campaign brief says ~1 minute; the deck says 3 minutes. Provide one validated figure from lab testing (state ambient start temperature and test method). Until then the site publishes no time.
2. **Drinking window.** Deck states 20 minutes. Needs test data.
3. **Activation steps.** Campaign brief: peel bottom sticker, press concave protrusion. Deck: press bottom cap/plunger to puncture pouch. Confirm the final consumer steps, any shake/wait instruction, orientation, and the warning text for the label.
4. **Safety evidence.** Exterior surface temperature during heating, seal integrity, leak/drop tests, pressure/venting behaviour, misuse cases (activating upside down, child access), and food-contact compliance for every material touching the beverage.
5. **Recipe & label.** Final ingredient list, allergen declaration (milk? soy? gluten?), nutrition panel, net beverage volume, shelf life, storage conditions, veg/non-veg mark, FSSAI licence number.
6. **Cocoa supplier claim.** Confirm Callebaut / Cacao Barry is in the final recipe and that the brand owner permits naming it on marketing.
7. **Regulatory status.** FSSAI licence/product approval; BIS and consumer-safety requirements; PESO and logistics clearance (including whether calcium oxide modules need special handling in transport). Provide documents and dates for each.
8. **Disposal/recycling guidance** for a used can containing a spent calcium hydroxide module. Do not claim "recyclable" without evidence.
