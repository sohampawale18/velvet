About Velvet Ember

We’re building a self-heating hot chocolate can for India, and we’re doing it in the open.

Why we’re making it

Hot chocolate is comfort in a cup, but it usually needs a kitchen or a café. Velvet Ember brings that warm moment into a portable can, pairing a familiar, indulgent drink with a built-in heating chamber.

Our mission is simple: make warm hot chocolate easier to enjoy beyond the home or café.

We care about four things: convenience, a drink that genuinely tastes good, being transparent about what’s proven and what isn’t, and taking product safety seriously.
